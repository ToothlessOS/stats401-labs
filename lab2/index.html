Lab 2: Multivariate Visualization with D3

Design Justification

I choose to put population on the x axis, the development level on the axis and visualize the temperature using colors and the region using shapes, for the following reasons:

  1. I expect a correlation between larger polution and higher development level. Therefore, I put the two on x axis and y axis respectively to clearly visualize this trend.
  2. After illustrating the different regions with different shapes, we can easily compare the populations and development levels of different regions. For example, the southern cities are concentrated on the buttom-left of the figure - suggesting that the region is relatively underdeveloped with smaller population.
  3. While representing different temperature with colors is a intuitive choice and people can take in that information at a glance.
  4. The tooltip allow the users to view the detailed data for each city easily.

Overall, I think this visualization design do a good job in showing the trends between the different variables in the cities dataset. It is also very readable.

Part 3. Critique the Visualization

Strength

1. Sankey diagram is the right diagram for the task - identify the switches and where exactly there are going
2. Though being static, the diagram (`ref2.webp`) focuses on the switches from MSFS2020 - the most interesting part of the data - how are the users upgrading from the last gen simulator that used to have to largest market share?
3. The color shckeme is well designed (`ref2.webp`) and makes the migration patterns very clear, especially the use of color gradients between MSFS2020 and MSFS2024.

Weaknesses

1. Overplotting if trying to percent all data at once(`ref.png`)
2. Add tooltip and interactivity to facilitate the exploration
3. Effectiveness: We can show both the changes in market shares as a whole and the migration status; Use of level of details toggle to switch between the two.

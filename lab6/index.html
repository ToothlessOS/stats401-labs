About the design

Visual channels

I use high constrast colors for different continents and easy to understand glyphs to indicate the growth trend of GDP. The sizes of the cells intuitively represent each country's GDP and enables comparison. To avoid clustering, the country label and/or the growth glyphs are hidden for cells that are too small. A prompt is included in the legend for this, asking the user to use the hovering tooltip for hidden information.

Comparison between squarify and binary -

Both methods achieve relatively good readability of the hierarchy. "Squarify" pushes the cells to be more squared, while "binary" pushes the cells to follow a balanced binary tree. In this case, both successfully avoid cells that are too thin and hurts readability of the hierarchy. They also make each levels of hierarchy distinctive by adding the approproiate white spaces.
